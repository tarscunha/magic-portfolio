summary: "My work focused on enhancing the Google Ads campaign management experience for enterprise clients in the travel industry. A core part of my role was designing results-oriented, information-dense user interfaces for managing large-scale campaigns. I led the effort to enable and tailor Performance Max, a cross-channel campaign type, for the travel vertical. This involved establishing a new top-level Travel channel in Google Ads to accommodate new verticals like Activities, Flights, and Car Rentals, ensuring a scalable and unified advertising structure beyond just Hotels."
